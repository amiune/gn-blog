Privacy Policy

Effective date: February 13, 2026

SB Inventory for Makers (“we”, “our”, or “the app”) is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what the iOS app stores and how it uses Apple’s services. The app is not intended for children under 13.

1. Information you store in the app

The app only processes data you enter for your own inventory:

  • Products: name, SKU, category, and notes.
  • Variants: size, color, material, quantities, and low-stock thresholds.
  • Materials (optional): name, quantity, unit, supplier, and low-stock thresholds.
  • Transactions: stock changes (added or removed), dates, and references to the product/variant they relate to.

We do not require a separate account beyond your Apple ID for iCloud.

2. Where data is stored

Inventory data is saved in your iCloud account using Apple’s CloudKit private database. It syncs only to devices signed in with the same Apple ID. The app does not provide multi-user or shared editing.

We do not run our own backend for your inventory. Storage and sync are handled by Apple’s iCloud infrastructure under your Apple ID.

3. Third-party services in the app

The app does not embed analytics SDKs, ads, or third-party trackers. The only external service involved in app data is Apple iCloud (CloudKit).

4. Website (if you use our marketing pages)

If you visit our public website, it may use standard tools such as Google Tag Manager or similar for aggregated traffic insight. That activity is separate from data inside the iOS app and is governed by the policies of those providers.

5. Export

You can export data to CSV from the app (e.g. products, variants, materials, or a combined report) for your own backups or spreadsheets.

6. Retention and deletion

Data remains in your iCloud while you use the app. You can delete items inside the app. To remove all app data from iCloud, use your Apple ID / iCloud settings or delete the app and associated iCloud data as described by Apple.

7. Changes

We may update this policy; the effective date at the top will change when we do. Please review this page occasionally.
