Features

Everything for the trip—nothing for the group chat

Itinerary, ideas, votes, reservations, packing, and expenses in one private workspace you share with travel companions.

📅

Day-by-day itinerary

Confirmed plans with times, places, notes, and maps handoff. The itinerary is the source of truth for calendar and PDF export.

✓

Ideas & voting

Anyone can suggest an activity. Support the ideas you like. Ties stay ties until an organizer confirms a winner into the itinerary.

🎫

Reservations

Keep flights, lodging, restaurants, and tickets together—confirmation codes, dates, and attachments, without forwarding emails.

$

Expenses that settle

Log who paid, who participated, and how to split. See balances and a settlement summary. Export CSV when you need it.

☁

Private iCloud share

Each trip is independently shareable through a private CloudKit zone. No public links, no developer server, no accounts.

🧳

Packing & lists

Shared checklists with assignees and checked state—packing, groceries, or whatever the group needs to remember.

↗

PDF, calendar & CSV

Export the confirmed itinerary as PDF or to Calendar. Optionally include the voting appendix. Expense CSV stays independent.

How it works

From first idea to a shared itinerary

1

Create the trip

Name, destination, dates, time zones, and currency. Start locally—iCloud is optional until you share.

2

Suggest and vote

Companions propose activities for a day. Support the ones you want. An organizer confirms winners into the itinerary.

3

Invite the group

Unlock Full Access to create shared trips. Accepting an invitation is always free—companions never need to buy.
