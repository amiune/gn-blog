Screenshot export guide

  1. Run Clean Job Tracker in the iOS Simulator — demo data is seeded automatically with dates relative to today.
  2. Capture each tab (Today, Calendar, Jobs, Clients) with Cmd+S or paste (Cmd+V) into the placeholders.
  3. Click Download All Screenshots for six PNGs at 1284 × 2778px (6.5″ display). Screenshots 5–6 are marketing-only (no app capture needed).
  4. Upload to App Store Connect under the 6.5″ iPhone screenshot slot.
  5. Filenames include the language code: e.g. CleanJobTracker-iPhone-Screenshot-de-1.png
