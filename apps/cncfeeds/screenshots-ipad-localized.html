📸 iPad screenshot export guide

  1. Add captures: Click a placeholder or paste (Cmd+V)
  2. Pick a language above to preview localized copy before exporting
  3. Click Download All Screenshots for six PNGs (5–6 are marketing-only)
  4. Click Download All Languages for all 30 PNGs (6 screenshots × 5 languages)
  5. Exported size 2048 × 2732px
  6. In App Store Connect, use 12.9″ iPad Pro (or equivalent) slot
  7. Filenames include the language code: e.g. CNCFeeds-iPad-Screenshot-de-1.png
