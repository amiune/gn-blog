📸 Screenshot Export Guide

  1. Add your screenshots: Click on a placeholder or paste (Cmd+V) to add your app screenshots
  2. Pick a language above to preview localized copy before exporting
  3. Click Download All Screenshots for the selected language, or Download All Languages for all localized PNGs (6 screenshots × 5 languages)
  4. Screenshots are exported at 1284 × 2778px (6.5" display)
  5. No alpha channel/transparency (App Store compliant)
  6. In App Store Connect, upload to the 6.5" iPhone section — other sizes are auto-generated
