Features

Everything you need to
manage your jobs

From scheduling to completion, Trade Job Tracker gives you a complete toolkit designed for how field service businesses actually work.

📋

Kanban Board

Visualize your workflow with drag-and-drop columns: Pending, In Progress, and Completed. See all your jobs at a glance.

📅

Calendar View

See your jobs on a monthly calendar. Plan ahead and never miss a scheduled service date again.

📊

Revenue Statistics

Track your revenue, payments, and job trends by week, month, or year. Know how your business is doing.

👥

Team Sharing

Invite team members to collaborate on jobs in real time. Everyone stays in sync through iCloud.

📎

File Attachments

Attach photos and PDFs to any job. Keep reference images, invoices, and notes all in one place.

☁️

iCloud Sync

Your data syncs seamlessly across all your Apple devices. Start on your iPhone, continue on your iPad.

Grow Your Business

Use calendar views and revenue statistics to spot trends and make better decisions.
