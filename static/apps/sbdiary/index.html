Everything US small business owners want to know about SB Diary.

SB Diary is a private daily journal app designed specifically for small business owners, solopreneurs, and entrepreneurs in the United States. It lets you capture daily wins, challenges, revenue, expenses, and your mood — all in one place on your iPhone. Think of it as your personal business diary that helps you reflect and grow.
SB Diary is free to download from the App Store. There are no hidden fees, no subscriptions required to start journaling, and no ads. You can start documenting your business journey right away without spending a dime.
Absolutely. SB Diary stores all your data locally on your iPhone. There are no cloud servers, no user accounts, and no third-party analytics. Your business journal entries, financial data, and mood logs never leave your device. Your data is protected by your device's built-in encryption.
Yes! Every journal entry lets you log daily revenue and expenses in US dollars. Over time, SB Diary shows you financial trends and summaries so you can see how your business is performing week over week and month over month — right alongside your journal notes.
Unlike generic journal or notes apps, SB Diary is purpose-built for business owners. It combines daily journaling with mood tracking, revenue/expense logging, category tagging (Sales, Marketing, Product, etc.), streak tracking, and business-specific insights — all in one streamlined interface designed for entrepreneurs.
