iPad screenshot export guide

  1. Run the app on an iPad Simulator (demo data seeds automatically).
  2. Capture Today, Calendar, Jobs, and Clients — paste with Cmd+V or click to upload.
  3. Click Download All Screenshots for six PNGs at 2048 × 2732px (12.9″ iPad Pro slot).
  4. Screenshots 5–6 are marketing-only — no app capture needed.
  5. Use landscape or portrait captures for slots 1–4 that match how you market the app on iPad.
  6. Filenames include the language code: e.g. CleanJobTracker-iPad-Screenshot-de-1.png
