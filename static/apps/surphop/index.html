Features

Progress you can see.
Feedback on the exact clip.

Private skill timelines, short videos, and invited-only video tips. No public feed. No extra account.

🎞

Learning timelines

One timeline per skill. Name it, then add short clips as you practice. Newest first, so the change is easy to see.

⏺

Short progress videos

Record in-app or pick from your library. Clips stay under 5 minutes and 100 MB — enough to show the work, not a lecture.

💬

Video tips on that clip

A teacher or friend replies with another short video attached to the exact check-in. Not a timeline-wide comment thread.

✉️

Invite-only sharing

Share one timeline through a private iCloud invitation. No public link, no searchable gallery, no anonymous viewers.

☁

Your iCloud, your videos

Progress lives in your private CloudKit database. Invited people can watch and reply without buying sharing — and without creating an account.

🔔

Know when they reply

When someone leaves a video comment on a timeline you’ve shared, you get an in-app notification — and a push alert if you allow it.

How it works

From a first clip to a private lesson

Three steps from “I’m practicing this” to a trusted person showing you what to try next.

1

Name the skill

Create a timeline for one thing you’re learning — fingerstyle, latte art, a language, a sport.

2

Record the work

Add short videos as you practice. Optional notes. The timeline becomes a private record of the change.

3

Invite someone you trust

Unlock lifetime sharing once. They open the timeline under Shared and reply on the exact clip — they never pay.
