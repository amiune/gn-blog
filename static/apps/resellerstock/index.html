Features

Built for buy–sell–ship workflows

Inventory, sales, fulfillment, and partner sharing in one private workspace—without a developer server.

▣

Inventory that searches

Track items with photos, SKUs, barcodes, categories, brands, costs, and locations. Find anything by name, note, or scan.

∥

Barcode scanning

Scan to open a matching item instantly, or create a new listing from an unknown code—no external catalog lookup.

$

Sales & estimated profit

Record revenue, marketplace fees, shipping, and cost of goods. Completing a sale adjusts stock atomically.

↗

Fulfillment tracking

Mark sales awaiting shipment, shipped, or delivered. Add carrier and tracking without changing stock or profit history.

☁

Share with a partner

Invite one sales partner through private iCloud sharing. Both of you can view, add, edit, and delete the whole workspace.

📎

Photos & documents

Attach product photos, receipts, invoices, and shipping labels. Files sync with your partner when sharing is on.

↗

CSV & ZIP export

Export inventory and sales as CSV anytime. Full Access unlocks ZIP archives with attachments and a manifest.
