Privacy Policy

Effective date: March 23, 2026

Colab Shopping List ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our shopping list application.

1. Information We Collect

Colab Shopping List stores only the data you actively enter into the app:

  • Shopping list items: Item names, checked/unchecked status, and optional prices you enter for each item.
  • Shopping lists: Active and archived lists with their creation and archive dates.
  • Item suggestions: A local record of items you've previously added, used to provide smart autocomplete suggestions.

We do not collect any personal data such as your name, email, location, or any identifiers.

2. How Your Data Is Stored

All data is stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework. Your data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to any server.

We do not operate any servers. All data resides entirely on your device. There is no cloud sync, no remote storage, and no external data transmission of any kind.

3. Third-Party Services

ColabShopping List does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. The app does not connect to the internet for any purpose related to your data.

4. Device Permissions

ColabShopping List does not request any special device permissions. The app does not access your camera, photo library, contacts, location, microphone, or any other device resources.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

Your data is stored on your device for as long as you use the app. You can delete individual items, archive lists, or clear all data (including suggestions and archived lists) from the app's Settings screen. To delete all data completely, you can simply remove the app from your device.

6. Children's Privacy

ColabShopping List does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under 13. The app is safe for users of all ages.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
