What We Do

We build tools that empower, not exploit

We help small businesses to save money and time and increase productivity by building tools that are tailored to their needs. In a world of subscription fatigue and AI hype, we offer something different: technology that respects your intelligence, your wallet, and your time. AI is great but not magic: it takes time and money to do something good, and we know small businesses can't afford to spend months learning and testing. That's where we come in: we help you create your app for free, then we try to monetise on the App Store. We also build open source software for data analytics and business predictions that you can download and use for free, and pay only if you need advice or support using it.
