Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 22, 2026

Introduction

SB Diary ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our Small Business Daily Journal application.

In Short: SB Diary stores all your data locally on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or have access to any of your personal information or journal entries.

Data Storage

📱

Local Storage Only

All your diary entries, business data, financial records, and preferences are stored exclusively on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework. Your data never leaves your device.

🔒

No Cloud Sync

We do not use iCloud, third-party cloud services, or any external servers to store or sync your data. Your journal remains private and under your complete control.

🚫

No Account Required

SB Diary does not require you to create an account, sign in, or provide any personal information to use the app.

Information We Collect

We collect absolutely no information. Specifically:

  • No personal identification information
  • No usage analytics or tracking
  • No crash reports sent to us
  • No advertising identifiers
  • No location data
  • No device information

Information You Store

The app allows you to store the following information locally on your device:

  • Daily journal entries and notes
  • Business mood and sentiment tracking
  • Business category tags
  • Revenue and expense records
  • Entry dates and timestamps

This information is stored only on your device and is not accessible to us or any third parties.

Data Security

Your data is protected by:

  • Device Security: Your data is protected by your device's passcode, Face ID, or Touch ID
  • iOS Encryption: iOS provides hardware-level encryption for all data stored on your device
  • App Sandbox: The app operates in iOS's secure sandbox environment, isolated from other apps

Data Deletion

You have complete control over your data:

  • Delete individual entries at any time from within the app
  • Uninstalling the app will permanently delete all associated data
  • No data remains on any server because no data is ever transmitted

Third-Party Services

SB Diary does not integrate with any third-party services, SDKs, or analytics platforms. The app functions entirely offline and does not make any network requests.

Children's Privacy

SB Diary does not knowingly collect information from children under 13. The app does not collect any information from anyone, regardless of age.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

Your Rights

Since we don't collect any data, there is no personal data to access, correct, or delete from our systems. All your data remains exclusively on your device under your complete control.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or our privacy practices, please contact us at:

support@sbdiary.app

Summary: Your privacy is paramount to us. SB Diary is designed from the ground up to keep your business journal completely private. We never see, collect, or store any of your data. It all stays on your device, always.
