QuietNote

Minimal notes, maximum clarity

iOS $2.99 — one-time purchase

Why QuietNote?

Note-taking apps have become bloated with features most people never use -- databases, kanban boards, AI writing assistants. QuietNote strips it all back to what matters: writing and finding your notes.

Fast, lightweight, and private. QuietNote launches instantly, uses minimal storage, and never sends your notes anywhere.

Key Features

Markdown Support

Write in Markdown with a beautiful live preview. Bold, italics, headings, lists, code blocks -- all supported.

Instant Search

Find any note in milliseconds with full-text search across all your notes.

Folders & Tags

Organize your notes your way with a simple folder structure and optional tags.

Dark Mode

A carefully designed dark theme that's easy on the eyes during late-night writing sessions.

Export Anywhere

Export notes as Markdown, plain text, or PDF. Your notes are never locked in.

Your Notes, Your Device

All notes are stored on-device with optional iCloud sync between your Apple devices. No accounts required. No servers involved. No data collection whatsoever.
