FocusBoard

Your personal task manager, reimagined

iOS $4.99 — one-time purchase

Why FocusBoard?

Most task managers today require an account, push you toward premium subscriptions, and sync your data to servers you don't control. FocusBoard takes a different approach.

Everything stays on your device. Your tasks are stored locally using Core Data, with optional iCloud sync between your own Apple devices. No third-party servers. No tracking.

Key Features

Smart Priority System

Automatically surfaces your most important tasks based on deadlines and your own priority rankings.

Focus Mode

Hide everything except the single task you should be working on right now.

Daily Review

A quick morning workflow to plan your day in under 2 minutes.

Widgets

See your top tasks right from your Home Screen or Lock Screen.

Shortcuts Integration

Automate task creation with Siri Shortcuts for seamless workflows.

Built with Privacy in Mind

FocusBoard collects zero analytics. There are no tracking SDKs, no crash reporters that phone home, and no account system. Your productivity data is yours alone.
