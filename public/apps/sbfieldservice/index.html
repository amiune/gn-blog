Features

Not a CRM.
A field notebook.

Built for solo operators who work from Notes, Photos, and memory—jobs, timeline, estimates, and payments in one offline-first app.

⚡

Fast job capture

Create a job with a title only—customer, address, and due date can wait. Saved on device instantly, synced when iCloud is available.

📷

Photos & timeline

Before, after, receipt, and issue photos on every job. Your work history reads like a timeline—not buried attachments.

🔧

Materials list

Track parts per job: quantity, estimated cost, purchased status, and store. See total material cost in the job summary.

📝

Simple estimates

Line items with tax, discount, and deposit. Share a professional estimate PDF through the iOS Share Sheet—no customer portal.

💵

Payment tracking

Record cash, card, transfer, or check payments. See estimate total, deposit, paid amount, and balance due—without payment processing.

☀️

Today view

Overdue, today, upcoming, and unscheduled jobs at a glance. Answer “what should I do today?” before you leave the driveway.

☁️

Private iCloud sync

Your data stays in your private CloudKit database—no team workspaces, no backend server, no subscription. Works offline.

How it works

Capture first, organize later

Three steps from a new job to a shared estimate—designed for standing in a driveway, not at a desk.

1

Create a job

Tap New Job, enter a title, and you’re done. Add customer, address, or due date whenever you have them.

2

Build the job

Add photos, notes, materials, and a simple estimate. Everything lives on one timeline in the job detail view.

3

Get paid & share

Log payments, mark the job paid, and share an estimate or job summary PDF via Messages, WhatsApp, or AirDrop.
